title: Decision records
description: The architecture decision records of the Xberg SDKs.
---

The SDKs keep their architecture decisions in [`adrs/`](https://github.com/xberg-io/sdks/tree/main/adrs), in MADR 4
as adrlint writes them. Org-wide decisions are recorded in the
[org decision log](https://github.com/xberg-io/agent-conventions/tree/main/adrs) and cited from here as
`org ADR-NNNN`. Add a row in the same change as a new record.
